About FitFuel

Delhi NCR's first AI-powered, 100% pure vegetarian nutrition platform — built at the intersection of real Indian kitchens, clinical nutrition science, and modern technology.

📖 Our Story

FitFuel began with a simple frustration: pure vegetarian eaters — whether by faith, lifestyle, or health goal — were stuck choosing between "healthy but boring" and "tasty but untracked." Fitness meal plans were built around chicken breast and whey. Traditional tiffin services didn't count macros. And nobody built specifically for Jain diners, Navratri fasting, or festival weeks.

We started PureVeg FitFuel Kitchen to close that gap — a single kitchen and platform that could serve a gym-goer chasing 42g of protein, a grandmother observing Ekadashi, a new mother rebuilding her iron levels, and a Jain family that needs zero onion-garlic, all from the same trusted, lab-tested menu.

Today FitFuel combines a chef-led pure vegetarian kitchen in Delhi NCR with an AI meal-planning engine, a food-photo macro scanner, and 60-minute delivery — so eating well stops being a trade-off.

🔭 Our Vision

To become India's most trusted pure vegetarian nutrition brand — the default choice for any household, athlete, or family that wants food that is honest about what's in it, without ever compromising on taste, tradition, or dietary belief.

🎯 Our Mission

To make accurate, personalized, pure vegetarian nutrition accessible to every Indian household — using AI to remove the guesswork from what to eat, and a chef-led kitchen to make sure it never stops tasting like home.

🌿 What FitFuel Provides

  • Personalized AI meal planning — Mifflin-St Jeor BMR/TDEE-based daily plans built around your goal, budget, and dietary needs.
  • AI food scanner — built into the AI Coach chat: photograph any dish and get 100% accurate, lab-verified macros, its best-for use case, and healthy alternatives — instantly.
  • Lab-tested pure vegetarian menu — breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks and drinks, accurate to within ±5% of stated macros.
  • Fasting, Jain & festival menus — Navratri, Ekadashi, Jain-certified (no onion-garlic, no root vegetables), and a month-wise Hindu festival calendar.
  • Packed products & Combo Builder — build-your-own multi-item packs with allergen exclusions.
  • Subscriptions — recurring meal plans with savings for individuals and families.
  • Progress tracking — a personal dashboard for streaks, macros and goals over time.
  • 60-minute delivery across Delhi NCR, with zero preservatives and no onion-garlic options on request.

🥦 Why 100% Pure Vegetarian

We serve one menu, cooked in one kind of kitchen — no meat, fish, or eggs, ever cross-contaminated. That means genuine peace of mind for religious vegetarians, Jain families, and anyone who simply eats plant-based, without having to double-check every order.

💛 Our Values

  • Accuracy over guesswork — every macro is lab-tested, not estimated.
  • Respect for dietary belief — Jain, Vrat and festival needs are built in, not bolted on.
  • No preservatives, transparent sourcing.
  • Technology in service of the kitchen, never replacing it.

Ingredients We Cook With

A pictorial guide to what actually goes into your FitFuel meals — where each ingredient comes from, why we use it, and the health benefits it brings to your plate.

🌾

Whole Grains & Millets

Rice · Wheat · Ragi · Jowar · Bajra · Quinoa
🍽️ Uses

Base for rotis, rice bowls, khichdi and porridge across breakfast, lunch and dinner meals.

⭐ Why We Use It

Millets like ragi and jowar carry a lower glycemic index than refined atta, so they release energy more slowly — a big part of how our Diabetes Support and Weight Loss meals are built.

💚 Health Benefits
  • Sustained energy release, fewer sugar spikes
  • Rich in fiber for gut & heart health
  • Naturally gluten-free options (ragi, jowar, bajra)
🫘

Legumes, Pulses & Soy

Moong · Chana · Rajma · Toor Dal · Soy Chunks · Tofu
🍽️ Uses

The backbone of our protein-forward dals, curries, soy-chunk sabzis and salad bowls.

⭐ Why We Use It

Paired with a whole grain, legumes give a complete amino-acid profile — the core science behind hitting real protein targets without meat.

💚 Health Benefits
  • High plant protein for muscle repair & satiety
  • Iron and folate — especially valuable for women's health
  • Low fat, high fiber, cholesterol-free
🧀

Dairy — Paneer, Curd & Ghee

Paneer · Curd/Dahi · Milk · Desi Ghee
🍽️ Uses

Paneer gravies and grills, curd-based raitas and lassis, and ghee used in tempering and traditional cooking.

⭐ Why We Use It

Fresh dairy is one of the highest-density protein and calcium sources available to a pure vegetarian diet — and curd's live cultures support digestion.

💚 Health Benefits
  • 18g+ protein per 100g paneer
  • Calcium & Vitamin D for bone health
  • Curd supports gut microbiome balance
🥜

Nuts & Seeds

Almonds · Peanuts · Chia · Flaxseed · Makhana
🍽️ Uses

Toppings for bowls and smoothies, roasted snacks, and a core ingredient in our fasting and Navratri menu (makhana, peanuts).

⭐ Why We Use It

A concentrated source of healthy fats and micronutrients that's easy to portion precisely — important for our macro-accuracy promise.

💚 Health Benefits
  • Omega-3s (flax, chia) support heart & brain health
  • Healthy fats improve satiety
  • Magnesium & Vitamin E for skin and recovery
🥦

Vegetables & Leafy Greens

Spinach · Bottle Gourd · Seasonal Local Vegetables
🍽️ Uses

Sabzis, salads and stir-fries built around what's fresh and in season across Delhi NCR.

⭐ Why We Use It

Seasonal sourcing keeps nutrient density high and lets us build low-calorie, high-fiber volume into every plate.

💚 Health Benefits
  • High fiber for digestion and fullness
  • Vitamins A, C & K, folate and antioxidants
  • Naturally low calorie, high volume
🌿

Spices & Herbs

Turmeric · Cumin · Coriander · Ginger · Black Pepper
🍽️ Uses

Flavour and colour come entirely from real spices and herbs — never artificial colouring or flavour enhancers.

⭐ Why We Use It

Whole and freshly ground spices deliver both authentic taste and functional health properties used in Ayurvedic-inspired recipes.

💚 Health Benefits
  • Turmeric & ginger — anti-inflammatory properties
  • Cumin & coriander support digestion
  • Black pepper improves nutrient absorption
🕉️

Fasting & Vrat Ingredients

Sabudana · Kuttu (Buckwheat) · Singhara · Sendha Namak
🍽️ Uses

The foundation of our Navratri, Ekadashi and Jain fasting menus — sabudana khichdi, kuttu puri, singhara halwa.

⭐ Why We Use It

These are the only grains/salts permitted during religious fasting — we pair them with peanuts, curd and makhana so a fasting day still hits real protein.

💚 Health Benefits
  • Sabudana & singhara — quick, easily digestible energy
  • Kuttu is naturally gluten-free and fiber-rich
  • Sendha namak is minimally processed rock salt
🫒

Cooking Oils & Fats

Cold-Pressed Groundnut Oil · Mustard Oil · Desi Ghee
🍽️ Uses

Used for tempering, shallow-frying and finishing — quantity per dish is always shown in the fat macro on the meal card.

⭐ Why We Use It

Cold-pressed oils retain more natural nutrients than refined oils, and portion-controlled ghee adds flavour without excess saturated fat.

💚 Health Benefits
  • Mustard oil — heart-healthy monounsaturated fats
  • Ghee in moderation supports fat-soluble vitamin absorption
  • No trans fats, ever
